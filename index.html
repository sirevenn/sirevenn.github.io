HEALTHTECH · AUTOMATION

LabGuide AI

Helping patients in Ghana understand lab tests before they walk into a clinic.

Problem

Patients are handed lab request forms with little explanation — leading to anxiety, incorrect preparation, repeat visits, and unexpected costs.

Solution

A mobile-first web app translating lab tests into plain language: purpose, prep instructions, sample type, turnaround time, indicative pricing, and NHIS coverage — with intelligent search across official names, common names, and abbreviations, plus a directory of labs across Greater Accra and Tema.

Result

Shipped and live with no login or personal health data collected. Directly applies 6 years of lab experience to close a real information gap for patients.

COMPUTER VISION · MODEL

CropGuard AI

A tomato leaf disease detector for farmers and extension officers.

Problem

Farmers and agricultural extension officers need a fast, accessible way to identify tomato disease in the field before crop loss spreads.

Solution

Trained a ResNet50 transfer-learning classifier on 18,166 PlantVillage tomato leaf images across 9 diseases plus healthy leaves. Wrapped in a Streamlit app that returns confidence scores, severity, treatment recommendations, and a downloadable PDF report.

Result

94% accuracy, 0.93 macro F1 across 10 classes. Deployed and publicly testable with sample images provided.

DATA PIPELINE · MODEL

Crop Disease Presence Classifier

Predicting disease presence from pest and environmental field data.

Problem

Agricultural teams need to predict disease presence from structured field data — pest presence and environmental readings — not just leaf images, and the labeled data available is heavily imbalanced.

Solution

Built an end-to-end scikit-learn / imbalanced-learn pipeline: type-aware imputation and encoding, SMOTE oversampling to correct class imbalance, and a class-weighted Logistic Regression classifier — exported as a deployable .joblib model, not just a notebook.

Result

A reusable, production-style inference pipeline ready to score new, unlabeled field records.
